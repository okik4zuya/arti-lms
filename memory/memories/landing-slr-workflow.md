## Wording for titles, SEO and ads
- Use: "cara membuat artikel SLR dengan AI", "SLR PRISMA", "SLR Scopus", "PRISMA flow diagram". Everyday pain words stay as in [[landing-page-keywords]] (colloquial, positive framing: "referensi asli").
- Avoid: "slr ai" (airsoft and car results). Do not lead with a speed promise; if used, pair with "tanpa mengarang". Never promise to pass AI detectors or Scopus acceptance ("SLR tembus Scopus" is a searched phrase but ARTi cannot promise it).
- Competitor tools named in the market (Elicit, SciSpace, ChatGPT, NotebookLM, Scopus AI, VOSviewer): any comparison on the page needs verified facts per [[landing-page-concept]] (no unverified claims about other products).

## What ARTi can honestly say (from the ARTi-SLR skill description; verify against the skill and a real run before the page says it)
- PRISMA-compliant workflow for an SLR as the paper itself: title and RQ and PICO(C) framing, protocol registration, multi-database search, deduplication, two-gate screening with logged exclusion reasons, extraction, quality appraisal, PRISMA flow diagram, synthesis matrix, hand-off to ARTi-writing.
- Reuses `arti-lit` as the one literature database; PRISMA counts come from the funnel log (`slr/prisma-counts.md`), reconciled with `arti-lit` (working-preferences rule).
- The PRISMA flow diagram can be made with ARTi-figure (concrete, visible demo; the market shows demand for it: 52.7k and 9.2k videos).
- Limits to state: Scopus search is paywalled and login-gated, the researcher runs it (output analysed by Claude); acceptance is the journal's decision.

## Selling rules agreed (details in [[course-packages-decisions]])
- Sold only to licence holders, or as a package with ARTi Framework; the course alone gives no licence.
- Discount sits on the course side; the tool in a package is priced at the current tier (promo > perintis > normal) and takes a launch place like any ARTi purchase.
- Claim wording: "Diskon paket hanya berlaku bila keduanya dibeli bersamaan." Not "sekali saja"; no countdown.
- Pre-launch only with a visible chapter list showing Terbit / Belum; price end by a real criterion ("semua bab terbit"), a date only if it can be kept; proposal: do not sell before about a third of the chapters are published; refund wording for "promised chapter not published" needs a lawyer.
- Existing buyers buy the course inside `/app` while logged in (attaches to the right account).
